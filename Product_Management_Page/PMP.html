Project Management Professional (PMP)

The Project Management Professional (PMP) is a globally recognized certification for project managers. It demonstrates that an individual has the knowledge, skills, and experience to successfully lead and manage projects. The PMP certification is offered by the Project Management Institute (PMI) and requires candidates to meet specific eligibility criteria, including a certain amount of project management experience and education. To earn the PMP certification, candidates must pass a rigorous exam that tests their understanding of project management principles, practices, and techniques.

Benefits of PMP Certification

  • Increased earning potential
  • Enhanced career prospects
  • Improved project management skills
  • Global recognition and credibility
